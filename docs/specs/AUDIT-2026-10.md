# MedicaGrow Billing Operations App — Audit
_billing.medicagrow.com · reviewed October 23, 2026 · logical structure, calculation reliability, visual and format structure_

## Scope
- Reviewed every module in the browser as Owner: Dashboard, AR Follow-Up, EOB/ERA, Tracker, Tasks, To Do, Analytics and Settings.
- Out of scope: missing data entry and tasks not closed by the team (training issues), and the source code itself.
- AR aging snapshotted at the batch upload date is intentional and is not treated as an issue.
- Items marked VERIFY are inferred from how the screens behave. Ask Claude Code to confirm them in the code before changing anything.

## Build order
- **Phase 1 — Make the numbers trustworthy**: CALC-02 to CALC-06, LOGIC-04, LOGIC-05. Mostly calculation and validation fixes, no redesign.
- **Phase 2 — Fix the model**: LOGIC-01 to LOGIC-03, LOGIC-06 to LOGIC-10, LOGIC-13, LOGIC-14, CALC-07, CALC-09, CALC-11. AR assignments with schedules, terminal outcomes, task-type definitions, the shift day.

## Calculation reliability (CALC)
These make numbers on screen wrong. Fix these first, because every report, alert and future audit depends on them.

### CALC-02 [P1] Timer sessions run for days and land on one date
- **Evidence:** Single-day totals of 47.5h and 70.7h (David D), 71h (Max C), 70.3h and 69.4h (Sam D) in the Workload Planner hover details.
- **Impact:** Inflates Time and Productivity, Workload Planner, efficiency and over-capacity counts. Most red cells in the planner come from this.
- **Fix:** Auto-stop a timer at shift end or after 10h and flag it for correction within the existing 48h edit window. Split any session that crosses midnight across the days it covers. Add a 'runaway timer' flag to Suspicious Activity.

### CALC-03 [P1] [VERIFY] EOB 'Avg days to resolve' shows 2,419 days
- **Evidence:** EOB Overview card. No denial can be 6.6 years old in a 2026 system.
- **Impact:** The KPI is meaningless and hides the real resolution speed.
- **Fix:** Average only resolved entries, using resolved date minus received date. Exclude rows with missing dates. A null date is probably being treated as epoch or year 0.

### CALC-04 [P1] [VERIFY] Time logged on open tasks seems to be ignored
- **Evidence:** Derek V has 22h 35m and 3h 47m logged on open Eligibility Check tasks, yet shows 0 every day in the Workload Planner and does not appear in Time and Productivity.
- **Impact:** People who don't close tasks look idle, and the hours vanish from reporting.
- **Fix:** Count every timer session by its session date, whatever the task's status. Report 'not closed' separately.

### CALC-05 [P1] Workload projection copies today's load onto every future day
- **Evidence:** Andrew P: 79.8h of unconfigured AR on Oct 23 is repeated on Oct 26–30, giving 81h per day.
- **Impact:** Future capacity is wildly overstated, so '11 billers over capacity' is mostly noise.
- **Fix:** Project only recurring series from their recurrence pattern. Never project one-off tasks or unconfigured AR.

### CALC-06 [P1] Three productivity counts disagree
- **Evidence:** Mike D: 311 AR notes this month vs 248 units on Time and Productivity. Most billers show no units for Claim Follow-up despite AR hours. Claim progress (e.g. Andrew P 99%, 0 notes this month) has no time window.
- **Impact:** There is no single trustworthy number for AR output.
- **Fix:** For AR, derive units from work notes automatically (one claim counts once per day, any outcome) and remove manual entry. Make claim progress period-based. Show Blue (routed to PM) claims separately instead of dropping them from the biller's total.

### CALC-07 [P2] [VERIFY] PM progress is inflated by Blue claims routed to them
- **Evidence:** David D (PM): 822 claims at 11%. Max C (PM): 715 claims at 8%.
- **Impact:** PMs look far behind when they are holding practice-coordination items.
- **Fix:** Track Blue claims in a separate practice-coordination queue with its own age and count, outside the PM's AR progress.

### CALC-08 [P2] 'Overall efficiency 187%' reads as good when it means 87% over estimate
- **Evidence:** Time and Productivity card: 'Logged ÷ estimated'. 'Avg time per unit 68m' blends eligibility checks, postings and denials.
- **Impact:** Misleads at a glance. The blended average has no meaning.
- **Fix:** Rename it 'Time vs estimate' and colour anything over 110% as over. Show time per unit only within a task type.

### CALC-09 [P2] 'Closed without count' fires 664 times
- **Evidence:** Suspicious Activity, October.
- **Impact:** The flag is noise because task types like Clinic Inquiry and Report have no natural unit, so real problems get buried.
- **Fix:** Add 'unit required' to each task type and flag only those.

### CALC-10 [P2] Resource Requirements cards rest on 3 practices
- **Evidence:** '2 understaffed, 1 adequate, 84.7h shortfall' while most practices show 'No requirement set'.
- **Impact:** The headline suggests the whole company is covered.
- **Fix:** Show coverage first, for example '3 of 29 practices have requirements set'.

### CALC-11 [P2] Timely-filing alert uses a fixed 90–120 day window
- **Evidence:** Smart Alerts: '17 claims approaching timely filing risk — 90–120 days old'.
- **Impact:** Payer limits range from 90 days to over a year. Claims past 120 days are not alerted. Because AR aging is a snapshot at upload, a claim can cross its deadline between uploads without any alert.
- **Fix:** Store a timely-filing limit per payer (with a default). Compute each claim's deadline from DOS against today's date, separately from the aging snapshot, and alert a set number of days before it.

### CALC-12 [P2] Tracker is fully manual and unused
- **Evidence:** Practice Health Tracker: 0 practices scored for October.
- **Impact:** The health score never exists, even though most inputs are already in the app.
- **Fix:** Auto-fill C (denials from EOB), D (AR over 90 days from batches) and E (follow-up compliance from AR notes). Keep A, B, F, G and H manual until their data exists.

## Logical structure (LOGIC)
These are design gaps where the app's model doesn't match how the work actually happens.

### LOGIC-01 [P1] AR is set up as one task per day instead of one assignment
- **Evidence:** Andrew P has 17 AR tasks for Howard Kessler DC, one per working day, none with daily hours. 30 'AR tasks unconfigured' company-wide.
- **Impact:** No pace, no target-date check, and the Workload Planner can't place AR work.
- **Fix:** One AR assignment per biller per batch, with claims, estimated minutes per claim, a target date, and a practice AR schedule (fixed days and hours, or flexible weekly hours). Required daily pace = remaining claims ÷ remaining scheduled days, recalculated daily.

### LOGIC-02 [P1] [VERIFY] Batches can close with most claims unworked
- **Evidence:** Howard Kessler DC closed at 21% complete (1,112 claims). Live wellness closed at 64%.
- **Impact:** Remaining red claims drop out of every queue and dashboard. This is potentially lost money.
- **Fix:** Require a close reason. Carry unresolved claims into the next batch, or list them in a 'closed with open claims' report.

### LOGIC-03 [P1] No end state for claims that won't be paid
- **Evidence:** Claim with Provider OON note: 'claims before 01/26/2026 won't be covered.' It stays Blue, with no follow-up date, assigned to Max C.
- **Impact:** Dead claims sit in AR forever and distort balance, aging and progress.
- **Fix:** Add terminal outcomes: 'Write-off recommended' (needs PM or Owner approval) and 'Transferred to patient'. Approved claims leave active AR and are reported separately.

### LOGIC-04 [P1] Structured notes save with blanks, and outcome defaults to 'Paid'
- **Evidence:** A saved note reads 'Denied on ____ for Provider Out-Of-Network'. The 'Log new note' form opens with Outcome type = Paid.
- **Impact:** Incomplete notes, and an accidental 'Paid' can turn a claim green.
- **Fix:** No default outcome. Make the fields required for the chosen outcome, and block save while any template blank is empty.

### LOGIC-05 [P2] Follow-up dates accept past or mistyped years
- **Evidence:** My Queue shows a follow-up of Sep 18, 2025 among Sep 2026 dates.
- **Impact:** The claim shows as overdue for a year and pollutes overdue counts.
- **Fix:** Follow-up must be today or later, and no more than 90 days out without confirmation.

### LOGIC-06 [P2] [VERIFY] Blue-claim routing and practices without a PM
- **Evidence:** Amanda Emerson's Primary PM is Max C, yet several Blue (Pt Resp) claims are assigned to Vishal. The Owner's AR queue holds 210 claims. Advanced Counseling and others have no Primary PM.
- **Impact:** Work silently piles up on the Owner.
- **Fix:** Confirm the routing order in code (practice PM, then batch owner, then Owner). Block batch upload for practices with no Primary PM. Show an 'unrouted' warning instead of falling back to the Owner.

### LOGIC-07 [P2] [VERIFY] AR and EOB denials are separate silos
- **Evidence:** EOB holds 696 unresolved denials. AR batches carry their own denial notes. Nothing on the AR claim screen shows a matching EOB entry.
- **Impact:** The same denial can be worked twice, or the two records can contradict each other.
- **Fix:** Match on claim number (or patient + DOS + CPT). Show the linked EOB entry on the AR claim and the reverse, and let one resolution close both.

### LOGIC-08 [P2] [VERIFY] Task statuses and recurring instances don't reflect reality
- **Evidence:** 'In process' is 0 for everyone. Overdue open tasks: Derek V 103, Ken P 185, Vance C 79, Andrew P 67. Instances #1 to #11 of 'Eligibility Check — Live wellness' are all open at once, although the design generates one instance at a time. All Tasks lists 605 open items, mostly repeats of the same series.
- **Impact:** You can't tell what's being worked right now, and the overdue counts are unusable.
- **Fix:** Set 'In process' automatically when a timer starts. Missed recurring instances older than X days should auto-mark as 'Missed' (counted in the audit) rather than stay open.

### LOGIC-09 [P2] [VERIFY] The work day crosses midnight but the app uses calendar days
- **Evidence:** My Day shows Owner blocks from 16:30 to 02:00 split across two dates. All daily totals cut at 00:00.
- **Impact:** One shift's work is split across two 'days' in the planner, productivity and pace calculations.
- **Fix:** Define a company 'shift day' (for example 16:00–04:00 IST, attributed to the US date) and use it for every daily calculation and view.

### LOGIC-10 [P2] Task types have no definition behind them
- **Evidence:** 18 task types with name and order only. 'Charge Entry & Eligibility Check' overlaps two other types.
- **Impact:** Units, estimates and audit checks can't be standardized, and eligibility reporting is split across two types.
- **Fix:** Give each task type a unit label, unit required yes/no, default minutes per unit, an amount-tracked flag, and (later) the auditor checklist. Split the combined type.

### LOGIC-13 [P1] No forward view of each biller's free capacity for AR planning
- **Evidence:** The Workload Planner shows assigned hours per day but not how much free time each biller has over the coming weeks, or whether a new AR batch would fit.
- **Impact:** PMs assign AR by guesswork, so target dates slip or billers get overloaded.
- **Fix:** Free hours = shift hours minus leave and holidays, recurring work (projected from actual average time, not estimates), committed AR and one-off tasks. Show it by week for the next 4–6 weeks. Add an assignment helper: enter claims, minutes per claim, practice schedule and target date; it lists billers with access to that practice, their free hours on scheduled days before the target, and whether the work fits. Accepting a plan reserves the hours as committed AR.

### LOGIC-14 [P1] No view of the task setup itself (series), only of instances
- **Evidence:** Recurring series can only be opened through 'Edit series' on one instance, in a modal. No page lists each practice's series with assignee, frequency and time. A one-off Claim Follow-up task has no practice set.
- **Impact:** A PM or Owner can't see what work each project is supposed to get, who owns it, how many hours it takes per month, or what's unassigned or missing.
- **Fix:** Add a Task Plan page listing series (not instances), grouped by practice or by person: task type, assignee, frequency, minutes per instance, monthly hours (estimated vs actual average), open backlog, next due, status (active/paused). Add a gaps panel (no assignee, assignee lacks practice access or is inactive, duplicate series, practices missing a required task type, one-off tasks with no practice) and inline actions (reassign, change frequency, pause). This same list serves as the auditor's required-task blueprint.

### LOGIC-11 [P3] To Do team view includes billers
- **Evidence:** Team To Dos lists all billers with 0% completion. Max C has 176 overdue to-dos.
- **Impact:** To Do is meant for the Owner and PMs, so this is noise. A 0% completion rate for someone with nothing assigned is misleading.
- **Fix:** Limit the view to Owner and PMs, and show '—' when nothing is assigned. Review Max C's backlog.

### LOGIC-12 [P3] [VERIFY] Non-practice entities sit in the practice list
- **Evidence:** CVBUSA, Anjali and Dipty are listed as practices with no batches or PM.
- **Impact:** They clutter every practice filter and the practice-count KPIs.
- **Fix:** Add a type field (Practice / Client group / Internal) and filter KPIs by it.

## Visual and format structure (UX)
These slow people down every day. They're cheaper to fix after the data is right.

### UX-01 [P2] Filters stack full width and push the work below the fold
- **Evidence:** All Tasks has 8 full-width filter rows before the table. AR My Queue and EOB Overview have 5–6.
- **Impact:** Billers scroll before seeing a single claim or task.
- **Fix:** One compact filter bar (3–4 key filters inline) plus a 'More filters' drawer, with saved views per role.

### UX-02 [P2] Every role lands on the same home page
- **Evidence:** The Owner home shows Tasks 'Open 0 / Overdue 0' (Owner's own tasks) while the team has 700+ open.
- **Impact:** Nobody gets a 'what do I do now' view.
- **Fix:** Biller home: today's tasks, AR pace for each assignment, overdue follow-ups. PM home: team today, data-health issues, Blue queue, assignments behind pace. Owner home: practice health, exceptions and audit results.

### UX-03 [P2] The Workload Planner is a 31-day grid of bare numbers
- **Evidence:** Weekends included, no plan vs actual, meaning hidden in hover text.
- **Impact:** Hard to read and act on.
- **Fix:** Three tabs: Data health, Team week (Mon–Fri, actual/planned per day), AR plans (pace per assignment). Clicking a biller opens a practice-by-day view.

### UX-04 [P3] Two different totals sit side by side
- **Evidence:** Batch header shows $20,487.00 total balance; the table shows $13,543.78 (excluding 0–30 days).
- **Impact:** Users ask which one is right.
- **Fix:** Pick one default (actionable), and show the other as a labelled secondary figure.

### UX-05 [P3] Auto-generated messages read awkwardly
- **Evidence:** 'over capacity on Friday and Monday and Tuesday and Wednesday and Thursday and Friday'.
- **Impact:** Hard to scan.
- **Fix:** Use date ranges ('Oct 23–30') and one line per person with counts.

### UX-06 [P3] Navigation duplicates and placeholders
- **Evidence:** 'AR Follow-Up' and its 'Practices' sub-item open the same page. 'Eligibility — Soon' is visible to all.
- **Impact:** Minor confusion.
- **Fix:** Merge the duplicate entries and hide unreleased modules from billers.

## Keep as is
- The work-note audit trail is permanent (add-only), and notes are generated from structured outcomes.
- Blue/Red/Green status categories and the reassignment to PMs are a strong base.
- Batch Analysis and Smart Alerts (denial patterns by payer) are genuinely useful.
- Suspicious Activity and Session Log already exist, so the timer fixes only need new rules, not new screens.
